# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

FDPM contributors are expected to make technical collaboration safe,
specific, and respectful. Good participation includes:

- discussing ideas and evidence without attacking people;
- giving actionable feedback and acknowledging uncertainty;
- respecting different backgrounds, identities, abilities, and experience;
- accepting correction and repairing harm;
- protecting confidential security and personal information;
- making room for newcomers to ask direct questions.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, doxxing, and retaliation are not acceptable. Deliberately
misrepresenting test evidence or concealing known safety defects is also
incompatible with this project's verification culture.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
release channels, and private project communications. It also applies when a
participant represents the project in public.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Send conduct reports privately to `pedroanisio@arc4d3.com`. Include the
relevant links or records, the behavior observed, and any immediate safety
concern. Do not use the security vulnerability channel for ordinary technical
disagreements.

The maintainer will limit disclosure to the people needed to review the
report, avoid conflicts of interest where possible, and communicate the
outcome to the reporter. Confidentiality cannot be absolute when law or an
immediate safety risk requires disclosure.

## Enforcement

Responses are proportional to impact and pattern. They may include a private
correction, content moderation, a warning, temporary participation limits, or
a permanent ban. A person subject to an action may request one reconsideration
by providing relevant facts that were not considered initially.

Project decisions made under this policy are documented privately when public
documentation would expose a reporter or sensitive personal information.
