## Active blockers (2)

- `blocker:resource-size-cap-design` _(Medium)_ — FDPM\_MCP\_MAX\_RESOURCE\_BYTES default needs operator agreement: 1 MiB is friendly to LLM context budgets but rejects most real PDF outputs. Options: (a) 1 MiB hard cap with --enable-large-resources opt-in; (b) different caps per content\_type (text=1MB, binary=10MB); (c) no cap, document the risk. Decision blocks p1-sizecap.
- `blocker:codemod-byte-equal-strategy` _(Medium)_ — p4-codemod-write's byte-equal gate may legitimately fail for SPECs where the legacy compareSectionNumbers ordering differs subtly from DFS-of-(parent\_node\_id, position). Need to decide: (a) hand-fix divergent SPECs and document the fix, (b) accept a short list of intentionally-divergent SPECs with explanation, (c) discover none diverge in practice. Resolution requires running the codemod against the corpus first.
